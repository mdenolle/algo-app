🎙️ Record Algo's voice

Algo talks with a kid's voice: yours! Read each line out loud with a big smile, like you're talking to a friend.

1. Press the red Record button, wait for "Go!", say the line, press Stop.
2. Press Play to hear it. Not happy? Record again.
3. Press Save: the file goes to your Downloads folder.
4. A grown-up moves the files into algo-app/voice/clips/ and runs npm run voice. Done!
